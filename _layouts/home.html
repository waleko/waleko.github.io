Researching LLMs for software engineering, reasoning, and environment setup at JetBrains.

I am a Junior Researcher at JetBrains, where I work on improving LLMs for SWE tasks with reinforcement learning and rigorous evaluation. I am also pursuing a Master's degree in Data Science under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Dmitry Vetrov at Constructor University in Bremen, Germany.

My work spans agentic coding systems, repository-level reasoning, long-context benchmarking, and practical research engineering. Previously, I worked in a research group under the supervision of Andrey Ustyuzhanin and received distinctions including a silver medal at NWERC 2023 and a first prize at IMC 2023.
