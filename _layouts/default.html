Hi! I am a Junior Researcher at JetBrains, working on improving LLMs on software-engineering tasks using Reinforcement Learning. I am pursuing a Master's degree in Data Science under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Dmitry Vetrov at Constructor University in Bremen, Germany.

I earned a silver medal at the ICPC Northwestern Europe Regional Contest (NWERC 2023) and a first prize award at the International Mathematics Competition for University Students (IMC 2023). During my Bachelor's studies, I worked in a research group supervised by Andrey Ustyuzhanin.
