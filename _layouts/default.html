Alexander Kovrigin

I am a Junior Researcher at JetBrains, working on improving LLMs on software engineering tasks with reinforcement learning. I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Data Science under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Dmitry Vetrov at Constructor University in Bremen, Germany.

I earned a silver medal at ICPC Northwestern Europe Regional Contest (NWERC 2023) and received first prize at the International Mathematics Competition for University Students (IMC 2023). During my Bachelor's studies, I worked in a research group under the supervision of Andrey Ustyuzhanin.
