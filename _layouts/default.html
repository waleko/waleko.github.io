Hi! I am a Research Engineer at JetBrains Research, where I work on improving large language models for software-engineering tasks using reinforcement learning. I am also pursuing a Master's degree in Data Science under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Dmitry Vetrov at Constructor University in Bremen, Germany.

I earned a silver medal at the ICPC Northwestern Europe Regional Contest (NWERC 2023) and a first prize at the International Mathematics Competition for University Students (IMC 2023). During my Bachelor's I worked in a research group led by Andrey Ustyuzhanin.
