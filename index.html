Alexander Kovrigin

I'm an ML researcher at JetBrains in Munich, working on RL post-training for language models and coding agents.

My recent research is on how token budgets in RL affect a model's reasoning, and on multi-task learning and step-level LLM routing. Before that, I worked on ML for software engineering, including automated environment setup and repository-level benchmarks. I did my B.Sc. in Computer Science and M.Sc. in Data Science at Constructor University.

I also have a background in competitive programming and math competitions (NWERC silver medal, IMC first prize) and occasionally teach at the ACTS camp.
