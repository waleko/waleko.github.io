I'm an ML Researcher at JetBrains in Munich, where I work on improving large language models for software‑engineering tasks with reinforcement learning.

I completed a Master's in Data Science under Prof. Dr. Dmitry Vetrov at Constructor University in Bremen, Germany. I earned a silver medal at the ICPC NWERC 2023 and a first prize at the IMC 2023.

During my Bachelor's I worked in a research group led by Andrey Ustyuzhanin. My work since has spanned reinforcement learning for coding agents, environment‑setup automation, and long‑context benchmarks for code.
